Sonic Mania (Data.rsdk)

Use the Data.rsdk from your own copy of Sonic Mania (for example the Steam version's install folder). Optional: the app can be used for the retro games alone.

Saves and settings

Uploading replaces the file with the same name. While a game is running, uploads are applied on the next start. A retro game's files download with the game's name in front (sonic1-SGame.bin); upload them under their own name (SGame.bin).

Retro games (Sonic 1, 2 and CD)

Without Sonic Mania's data the retro games are always offered.

Played from your own data packs. With the pack from Sonic Origins alone a game has no sound (Origins keeps its music elsewhere); add the pack of the earlier release and its music and sound effects are used. The earlier Sonic 1 and 2 packs and the mobile Sonic CD pack also play on their own. Takes effect after a restart.

With Sonic Mania's Data.rsdk installed: sounds the packs lack (all of them with the Origins pack alone, and the Drop Dash, Knuckles' and Amy's moves otherwise) and the zones Mania remixed (Green Hill, Chemical Plant, Oil Ocean, Stardust Speedway, Metallic Madness, the jingles).

For example Emerald Hill plays Green Hill and Casino Night plays Studiopolis. Takes effect after a restart.

Mania Plus content

Off by default, as the decompilation's licence requires. Takes effect after a restart.
